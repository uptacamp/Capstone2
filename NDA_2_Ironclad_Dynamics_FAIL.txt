                         NON-DISCLOSURE AGREEMENT
                    between Vantage Point Ventures LLC and Ironclad Dynamics Corp.


1. Purpose
Vantage Point Ventures LLC (“Discloser”) intends to disclose certain confidential information to Ironclad
Dynamics Corp. (“Recipient”) in connection with a potential commercial arrangement (the “Purpose”).
This Agreement sets forth the terms on which Recipient may receive and use such information.

2. Confidential Information
“Confidential Information” means all information disclosed by Discloser to Recipient in connection with
the Purpose, in any form, whether or not marked confidential.

3. Confidentiality Obligations; Perpetual Term
Recipient shall hold Confidential Information in confidence and shall not disclose it to any third party.
The obligations of confidentiality set forth in this Agreement shall survive indefinitely and shall have no
expiration date, regardless of termination of the Parties' business relationship.

4. License to Feedback and Related Improvements
Any Confidential Information, data, feedback, or related improvements shared by Discloser in
connection with the Purpose shall be deemed automatically and irrevocably licensed to Recipient, on a
worldwide, royalty-free, perpetual basis, for Recipient to use, modify, and incorporate into its own
products and services without further consideration or consent from Discloser.

5. Exclusivity
During the Term of this Agreement and for twelve (12) months thereafter, Discloser shall not, directly or
indirectly, discuss, negotiate, or enter into any agreement with any third party concerning the subject
matter of the Purpose without the prior written consent of Recipient.

6. Limitation of Liability
In no event shall Recipient be liable to Discloser for any damages, losses, or claims arising out of or
relating to this Agreement, including damages arising from Recipient's breach of its confidentiality
obligations hereunder. Discloser's liability under this Agreement shall not be limited or capped in any
respect.

7. Audit Rights
Recipient may, at any time and without prior notice, inspect and audit Discloser's facilities, systems,
and records to verify compliance with this Agreement. Discloser shall provide Recipient and its
representatives with unrestricted access for purposes of such audit.

8. Publicity
Recipient may issue press releases, marketing materials, or public statements referencing Discloser,
the existence of this Agreement, or the Purpose, without obtaining Discloser's prior consent.
9. Amendment
Recipient may amend this Agreement at any time by posting a revised version on its corporate website.
Discloser's continued disclosure of Confidential Information after such posting shall constitute
acceptance of the amended terms.

10. Governing Law and Dispute Resolution
This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of the Republic of Novaterra, and any dispute arising
under this Agreement shall be resolved by binding arbitration seated exclusively in Novaterra City, with
no right of appeal to any court.
